01

Watch + toolbar — the instrument panel

The bar is the app's primary navigation and it has no surface of its own, so its icon contrast is whatever the page happens to be: 10.35:1 over a white page, 1.55:1 over the video frame behind it, 1.10:1 in dark scheme on a light page. The refresh gives the bar its own material so the number is fixed at 10:1 by the token. The page here is drawn without the injected glass CSS — that is the case the bar has to survive.

03

Library drawer — a glass panel, and a first run

Both frames are drawn in dark scheme (the shipping default); the captured light-scheme drawer is in current-ui/before-library-drawer.png. The drawer's Android fallback is deliberately near-opaque (frost, 0.95, no blur), so it can only read as a layer if the scrim does its job — and the scrim is the problem: dark:bg-gray-600/50 raises a dark page from #0f0f0f to #2d3239 instead of dimming it, while bg-zinc-300/50 dims a light page by only 11 %. The proposed scrim dims by 26 % dark-on-light and keeps a dark page dark. With nothing saved the panel is also simply blank: LibraryModal passes no ListEmptyComponent.
