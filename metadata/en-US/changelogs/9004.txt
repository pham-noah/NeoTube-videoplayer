0.3.0

Upstream 0.7.11, merged
- YouTube Music's "media next" no longer adds songs to the app's queue.
- A feed preview no longer lands in watch history.
- TLS checks are enforced again.
- yt-dlp init errors are reported instead of failing silently.
- The header toolbar is configurable: every button can be shown, hidden or
  moved to the menu, and the bar can be reordered.

Updater
- "Get the update" downloads the APK in the app, with progress and a cancel
  that stops the transfer.
- The finished file goes straight to Android's package installer, and the app
  asks to be allowed as an install source when it has not been.
- A failed or truncated download says why and offers a retry.

Interface
- Settings search finds pages as well as settings, so "version" opens About.
- The update check reports its result where you tapped it, not in a toast.
- The version card in About copies the version for a bug report.
- A notice above the page answers YouTube's cookie wall, and explains the
  empty feed when watch history is off.
- The header toolbar can be rearranged; long-press it to open the editor.

Build
- scripts/build-release.mjs builds a release in one command from a short-path
  mirror, and audits the APK's version, ABI and signer.
