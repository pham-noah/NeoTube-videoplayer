YouTube and YouTube Music in a single app. No ads, plays in the background.
<ul><li>Manage watchlist, music library without login</li><li>Manage watch history without login</li><li>Download video</li><li>RSS feed reader for YouTube channels</li><li>Hide shorts</li><li>Live chat</li><li>Play original audio</li><li>Customize with CSS</li><li>Liquid Glass theme with four accent colours</li></ul>
<h2>How it works</h2>
<ul><li>Wrap https://m.youtube.com and https://music.youtube.com in Android webview</li><li>Inject code to block ads</li><li>Hook playback controls and support playing in background</li></ul>
